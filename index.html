About LASEA

A Legacy of
Inspired Learning

Founded in 1986, Lady Anusuya Singhania Educational Academy is a premier CBSE-affiliated institution dedicated to creating a strong foundation for lifelong success.

We combine rigorous academics with holistic development, nurturing students to become confident thinkers, empathetic leaders, and responsible citizens.

Our green campus supports focused learning with smart classrooms, advanced laboratories, sports facilities, and a safe residential environment for boarders.

Academic Programs

Holistic Education

A rich curriculum blending academic rigour with creative exploration, technology, and co-curricular activities.

Smart Classrooms

Interactive digital boards and modern teaching aids create an immersive learning environment that engages every student.

Science Laboratories

Well-equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Computer labs with latest apparatus for hands-on practical learning.

Abacus & Mental Math

Specialised programs for Classes III–VIII enhancing cognitive abilities, concentration, and mathematical proficiency.

Eco-Friendly Campus

Sprawling gardens, large play areas, and sustainable practices creating a green environment for healthy development.

Digital Parent Portal

Secure digital wallet for tracking attendance, report cards, performance graphs, and leave applications in real-time.

Residential Facility

Comfortable hostel accommodation for boys from Class VI to XII with round-the-clock care and supervision.
